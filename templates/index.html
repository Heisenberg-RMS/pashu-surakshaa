📞 Low-Connectivity IVR Voice Hotline (1962)

Designed for livestock farmers with basic 2G feature phones or low literacy. Farmers dial toll-free 1962 and follow spoken instructions using DTMF keypad numbers. The system instantly classifies symptoms, registers the report, and reads out bio-security advisories in local language.

Automated Voice Flow:
1️⃣ Welcome & Species Selection: Press 1 for Cattle/Buffalo, 2 for Goat/Sheep, 3 for Pig, 4 for Poultry.
2️⃣ Syndromic Hallmark: Press 1 if blisters & drooling (FMD); 2 if skin nodules (LSD); 3 if swollen throat (HS); 4 if sudden death (Anthrax).
3️⃣ Auto-Triage & Containment: Generates formal report in the central database, alerts the local veterinary hospital, and dictates immediate home-care measures.
